Do you need a loyalty program, or win back messages?

Both aim for repeat customers. They reach different people, cost different amounts, and are measured differently.

Updated 7 November 20263 min readBy the Loyalbase team
Short answer

A points program rewards customers who already come back. Win back messages reach the ones who stopped. For most small stores, win back messages are the better first step: they cost a message instead of a discount, they work on the customers you're actually losing, and you can see which ones brought sales back. Add a points program later if your regulars want rewards.

What a points program does well

  • Gives regulars a reason to keep choosing you over a competitor.
  • Tiers make your best customers feel recognised.
  • Gives new visitors a reason to create an account.

What a points program costs

Three costs, not one: the app fee, the rewards you fund, and the time to set it up and keep it running. Most loyalty apps also charge more as your order volume grows.

What a points program misses

A customer who has stopped buying doesn't visit your store, so they never see their points balance. Points are a reason to come back for people who are already coming back.

What win back messages do

  • Go straight to the person, on WhatsApp, from your shop.
  • Arrive when it matters: when that customer is later than their usual rhythm, or before their yearly occasion.
  • Need no discount to work for many customers.
  • Can be measured: who ordered after a message, compared with people who weren't messaged.

Which should you start with?

Start with win back messages if

  • most of your customers buy once or twice
  • you sell things people reorder
  • you don't want to fund ongoing discounts

Add a points program if

  • you have many frequent buyers who ask for rewards
  • your margins can carry ongoing discounts

Use both if

  • you want to reward your regulars and win back the ones drifting away. They don't conflict.

Questions

Can I use a loyalty program and Loyalbase together?

Yes. Loyalbase doesn't add a widget or change your checkout, so it runs alongside any points app.

Are loyalty programs worth it for small stores?

They can be, if you have enough frequent buyers to make points feel valuable and the margin to fund rewards. If most customers buy once or twice, start by bringing those customers back.

Do win back messages need a discount?

Not usually in the first message. A personal check in at the right moment works for many customers. Keep an offer for people who don't reply.
