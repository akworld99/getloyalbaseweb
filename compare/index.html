How Loyalbase compares

Points apps reward people who keep showing up. Email tools send what you build. Loyalbase finds the customers who stopped, and proves what came back.

Updated 7 November 2026
Short answer

Smile.io, LoyaltyLion and Yotpo are points and rewards programs. Klaviyo is an email and SMS platform. Loyalbase is different: it tells you which customers to bring back each day, gives you a WhatsApp message for each, and proves what came back with a held back group. It works alongside all of them.

Questions

Is Loyalbase a loyalty program?

No. There are no points, tiers or rewards. Loyalbase tells you which customers to bring back and why, writes the message, and proves what came back.

Can I use Loyalbase with Smile.io or Klaviyo?

Yes. Loyalbase doesn't change your store's checkout, theme or emails, so it runs alongside loyalty and email tools.
