Smile.io doesn't work with WooCommerce. Here are your options.

If you run a WooCommerce store and wanted Smile.io, you'll need something else. Which option fits depends on what you're really after: points, or customers ordering again.

Updated 4 November 20262 min readBy the Loyalbase team
Short answer

Smile.io supports Shopify, Shopify Plus, Shopify POS and BigCommerce, and its help centre says it doesn't integrate with WordPress, so WooCommerce stores can't use it. If you want points, use a WooCommerce loyalty plugin or a platform such as Yotpo that supports WooCommerce. If what you want is customers ordering again, Loyalbase finds the ones who stopped and gives you a WhatsApp message for each.

Questions

Does Smile.io work with WordPress or WooCommerce?

No. Smile.io's help centre lists Shopify, Shopify Plus, Shopify POS and BigCommerce as supported platforms and says it doesn't integrate with WordPress.

Can I use a points plugin and Loyalbase together?

Yes. Loyalbase doesn't change your checkout or add a widget, so it runs alongside any WooCommerce points plugin.

Is Loyalbase a loyalty program?

No. There are no points or rewards. It finds customers who stopped buying, gives you a message for each, and shows the sales that came back.
