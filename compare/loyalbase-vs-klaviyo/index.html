Loyalbase vs Klaviyo

They overlap less than you'd think. One is a full email platform. The other is a daily list of who to bring back, and proof of what came back.

Updated 7 November 20262 min readBy the Loyalbase team
Short answer

Klaviyo is a full email and SMS marketing platform: you build lists, flows and campaigns yourself, and the price rises with your contacts. Loyalbase is narrower: each morning it tells you which customers to bring back and why, gives you a WhatsApp message for each, and proves what came back against a held back group. Many stores use Klaviyo for newsletters and Loyalbase for personal win backs.

Choose Klaviyo if

  • email is your main channel and your list is large
  • you want newsletters and automated abandoned cart emails
  • you have someone with time to build and maintain flows

Choose Loyalbase if

  • your customers answer WhatsApp faster than email
  • you don't have time to build segments and flows
  • you want a short list each day and proof of what it brought back

Using both

Keep Klaviyo for newsletters and store emails. Use Loyalbase to find the people worth a personal message. You can download any Loyalbase list as a spreadsheet and import it into your email tool, or send it straight to Sendy.

Questions

Does Loyalbase send emails?

Not by itself. You can send any list to your own Sendy email list, or download it as a spreadsheet for your email tool.

Is Loyalbase cheaper than Klaviyo?

It depends on your list size. Klaviyo is free for very small lists and its price rises with your contacts. Loyalbase is $50 a month, flat, however many customers you have.

Does Klaviyo support WhatsApp?

Yes. Klaviyo announced WhatsApp in September 2025. You still build the flows and decide who to message yourself.
