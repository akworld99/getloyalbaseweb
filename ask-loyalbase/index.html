Ask your store questions in plain words

Who ordered before Eid last year but not since? Which day sells most? What do new customers buy first? Type it the way you'd say it, and get the answer from your own orders.

Updated 7 November 2026
Short answer

Ask Loyalbase lets a store owner type a question in plain words, or pick one of 47 ready-made questions, and get an answer from the store's own orders: a sentence, a table, or a list of customers ready to message. Every underlined word can be changed: dates, festivals, products, areas, amounts. The ready-made questions run without AI. Where AI answers are switched on, the AI only sees totals and product names, never customer names, phone numbers or emails.

How it works

  1. Type a question, or pick one. "who bought before christmas but didn't come back" works. So does "best days last month".
  2. Check the underlined words. Loyalbase fills in the dates, festivals, products, areas and amounts it heard. Tap any of them to change it. Blanks start filled with your own top product, area and channel.
  3. Get the answer. A plain sentence first, then a table or a list of customers.
  4. Act on it. Customer lists open like any other list: tap to WhatsApp one person, or download the list as a WhatsApp campaign file or a spreadsheet. Only people who said yes are included in downloads.

Festivals understood

Type a festival and Ask knows its dates, including moon-calendar festivals that move every year: Valentine's Day, Women's Day, Avurudu, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Children's Day, Ramadan, Eid ul-Fitr, Eid ul-Adha, Vesak, Deepavali, Thai Pongal, 11.11, Black Friday, Christmas and New Year's Eve. "Before Christmas" means the weeks when people order for it, not just the day itself. You can also pick your own dates.

Customers are also grouped by the festivals they order for, year by year, so you can find people who order for Eid or Christmas every year. How festival groups work →

Questions

Do I need AI to use Ask?

No. The 47 ready-made questions run straight on your numbers without AI. You type or pick a question, and Loyalbase matches it and fills in the details from your words.

What if my question isn't on the list?

Pick the closest one and change the underlined words. Where AI answers are switched on, you can also ask anything else in your own words.

Can I send a message to the customers in an answer?

Yes. A customer answer is a normal Loyalbase list. Tap to WhatsApp one person, or download it as a WhatsApp campaign file or spreadsheet with only people who said yes.

Does Ask work with my Meta ads and posts?

Yes, once Facebook is connected. Ask for Meta ad results by week or month, ads next to your sales, or your best posts by reach, likes or comments.

Is it in other languages?

Questions are in English today. Festival names like Avurudu, Eid, Vesak and Deepavali are understood, including common spellings.
