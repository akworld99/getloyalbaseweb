Privacy

Privacy

Two parts: what this website collects about you, and how the Loyalbase app handles your customers' data.

Updated 7 November 2026
Short answer

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and Formspree to receive the early access form. We don't sell personal data. The Loyalbase app never stores your customers' names, phone numbers, emails or addresses: those stay on your store, and Loyalbase works from order facts and scrambled IDs.

This website

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. It uses cookies and records things like pages viewed, device type and approximate location. You can block it with your browser settings or the Google Analytics opt out add on.

Early access form

When you fill in the early access form, we receive your email, store website and WhatsApp number through Formspree, which delivers it to our inbox. We use it only to contact you about Loyalbase.

WhatsApp links

Tapping a WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp, which is run by Meta. If you message us, we see your number and messages like any WhatsApp chat.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, they may receive your IP address when you visit.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep early access details for as long as we're in touch about Loyalbase, and delete them when you ask. You can ask to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you by emailing akmal@digisavvyteam.com. We don't sell personal data.

The Loyalbase app and your store's data

  • What Loyalbase stores: order facts such as dates, totals, products and delivery area, an occasion label such as "birthday", consent flags, and scrambled versions of phone numbers and emails.
  • What it never stores: customer names, phone numbers, emails, street addresses, or cake and card wording.
  • Scrambled IDs can't be reversed. The key used to scramble them is created on your website and never sent to Loyalbase.
  • Names stay on your store. When you open a customer, your browser fetches the name straight from your store using a pass that expires in five minutes. Names are never sent to Loyalbase or saved in your browser.
  • Every lookup is logged on your own store, so you can see each time a name was shown.
  • Anything that looks like contact details is refused. The Loyalbase servers reject data containing fields they don't expect.
  • You can delete your store from Loyalbase at any time with Delete this shop in settings.

Who we are

Loyalbase is a product of Digisavvy, Colombo, Sri Lanka. Questions about privacy: akmal@digisavvyteam.com.
