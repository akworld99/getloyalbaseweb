Questions owners ask

What is Loyalbase?

A customer retention app for WooCommerce and Shopify stores. It reads your orders, tells you who to message on WhatsApp and why, and proves how much came back. It also shows what's selling, which dates to plan for, and whether your ads paid.

How much does Loyalbase cost?

$50 a month, flat. The price doesn't rise as your orders grow, and early access is free while we set you up.

Do I need a points program?

No. Nothing to design, no discounts to fund. If you have one, keep it. They work together.

How does it know a customer is late?

It learns each customer's own rhythm. Someone who orders every three weeks is flagged after about six weeks without an order. Someone who orders twice a year isn't flagged for about nine months. Yearly occasions are spotted when the same person orders around the same date in different years.

Is customer data safe?

Names, phones, emails and addresses never leave your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled IDs it can't reverse. Names load from your store to your screen, and every lookup is logged.

How do I know the messages worked?

Every message is matched to paid orders within 60 days, counted once. And 1 in 10 people are held back and not messaged, so Results shows what your messages really added.

Does it work with my POS and branches?

Not yet. It's on the roadmap, starting with a sales file per branch and then direct sync for cloud POS systems. Walk in customers join the same lists when the till records a phone number.

Which countries does it work in?

Any country. Phone numbers are matched in full international form, and money shows in your own currency.

I use Klaviyo or a points app. Do I switch?

No. Keep them. Loyalbase finds the people who need a personal nudge and shows what it was worth.

Can I ask Loyalbase questions about my store?

Yes. Type a question in plain words, like "who ordered before Eid last year but not since", or pick one of over 40 ready-made questions. You get a sentence, a table or a list of customers from your own orders. The ready-made questions don't need AI, and any AI only ever sees totals.

Can it find customers who order every festival?

Yes. Customers are grouped by the festivals they order for, year by year: Christmas, Avurudu, Eid ul-Fitr, Eid ul-Adha, Vesak, Deepavali and Thai Pongal. Ask for anyone who ordered for a festival in two or more different years.

Does it spot business and corporate customers?

Yes. Anyone who orders with a company name, or from a work email address, is marked as a business customer, and people from the same company are counted together. Personal addresses like Gmail don't count, and the email never leaves your store.
