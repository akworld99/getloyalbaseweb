Questions owners ask

What is Loyalbase?

A customer retention app for WooCommerce and Shopify stores. It reads your orders, tells you who to message on WhatsApp and why, and proves how much came back. It also shows what's selling, which dates to plan for, and whether your ads paid.

Do I need a points program?

No. Nothing to design, no discounts to fund. If you have one, keep it. They work together.

How does it know a customer is late?

It learns each customer's own rhythm. A three week buyer is flagged after about four. A twice a year buyer isn't. Yearly occasions are spotted from repeated dates.

Is customer data safe?

Names, phones, emails and addresses never leave your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled IDs it can't reverse. Names load from your store to your screen, and every lookup is logged.

How do I know the messages worked?

Every message is matched to paid orders within 60 days, counted once. And 1 in 10 people are held back and not messaged, so Results shows what your messages really added.

Does it work with my POS and branches?

Not yet. It's on the roadmap, starting with a sales file per branch and then direct sync for cloud POS systems. Walk in customers join the same lists when the till records a phone number.

I use Klaviyo or a points app. Do I switch?

No. Keep them. Loyalbase finds the people who need a personal nudge and shows what it was worth.
