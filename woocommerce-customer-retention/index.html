Get more repeat customers from your WooCommerce store

WooCommerce keeps every order you've ever had. Loyalbase turns them into a short list each morning: who to message, why, and what it's worth.

Updated 4 November 2026
Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention plugin and app for WooCommerce. Install the plugin, press Connect, and your order history comes in. Every morning you see which customers are late for their next order, have a yearly occasion coming up, stopped at payment, or bought once and never came back, each with a WhatsApp message ready to send. Results shows which messages brought sales back.

Why WooCommerce stores lose repeat customers

WooCommerce is good at taking orders. It isn't built to tell you who stopped ordering. Its reports show totals: sales this month, top products, average order value. They don't tell you that a customer who used to buy every three weeks hasn't ordered in two months.

Customers rarely cancel. They drift to a competitor, a marketplace or the supermarket, and most owners only notice when the sales line goes flat. By then a friendly message is much less likely to work.

Seven lists, refreshed every day. Each person comes with the reason they're on the list and what one more order from them is worth.

How it works

  1. Install the Loyalbase plugin. In WordPress, go to Plugins, then Add New, then Upload Plugin. The download already knows your account.
  2. Press Connect to Loyalbase. Approve once. Your store creates its own secret key, which never leaves your website.
  3. Your order history comes in. It sends in the background with a live count while the Loyalbase page is open. Big stores take minutes, not hours.
  4. Open Today. Up to three jobs a morning, on your phone or laptop. Tap a customer and WhatsApp opens with the message written.

After that, Loyalbase updates after new orders and again every night.

What the plugin sends, and what it never sends

Sent to Loyalbase

  • Order dates and amounts
  • Products bought
  • Delivery area
  • An occasion label such as "birthday", worked out on your site
  • Scrambled versions of the phone and email, so repeat customers can be recognised

Never sent

  • Names
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Street addresses
  • Cake or card wording

The key used to scramble phone numbers and emails is created on your website and never sent, so Loyalbase can't turn them back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser fetches their name straight from your store using a pass that expires in five minutes, and every lookup is logged in WordPress. Read more on our privacy page.

More than a list

  • Sales: returning and new customers by month, best sellers, products rising and falling, what's bought together, and which discount codes bring people back.
  • Dates to plan for: big sale and gifting dates with last year's numbers and the date your ads should go live.
  • Marketing: connect Facebook and Google Analytics to see ad spend, posts and website visits next to the sales that really came in.
  • Bulk sends: download any list as a WhatsApp campaign file or a spreadsheet, or send it to your Sendy email list. Only people who said yes are included.
  • WhatsApp tick box at checkout: an optional box that lets customers say yes to WhatsApp messages. The answer is saved on the order as their consent record.
  • Proof: 1 in 10 people on the bring back lists are held back, so Results shows what your messages really added. How that works.

Questions

Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. If you can install a WordPress plugin, you can set up Loyalbase. Upload the plugin, activate it, and press Connect to Loyalbase.

Will it slow down my store?

The plugin sends order facts in the background in small batches. The only thing it adds to your checkout is the optional WhatsApp tick box, and you can leave that switched off.

What happens if I uninstall the plugin?

Uninstalling removes Loyalbase's settings and keys from your site. Customers' WhatsApp consent stays saved on their orders, because it's their record. To remove your store's data from Loyalbase as well, use Delete this shop in Loyalbase settings.

Does it work with my email or loyalty plugin?

Yes. Loyalbase doesn't change your checkout, prices or emails, so it runs alongside points plugins and email tools.

How much does it cost?

$50 a month, flat, whatever your order volume. Early access is free while we set you up.
