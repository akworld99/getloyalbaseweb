Get cake customers back for every birthday, not just one

Most cake customers order for a reason: a birthday, an anniversary, Eid, Christmas. Loyalbase remembers the date for you and tells you who to message, two weeks before.

Updated 7 November 20264 min readBy the Loyalbase team
Birthday in 12 daysAnna P. · ordered 14 Mar last year
Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for bakeries and cake shops that sell on WooCommerce (Shopify is in early access). It reads your orders, spots customers who order around the same date every year, regulars who have gone quiet and festival buyers who didn't come back, and gives you a WhatsApp message for each. Cake wording is turned into a label like "birthday" on your own website, so the message itself never leaves your store.

How cake customers come back

A bakery has two kinds of regulars. The first orders every week or two: office treats, bread, a Friday dessert. The second orders once or twice a year, always for the same reason, and is just as valuable. If you don't remind them, they order from whoever shows up first on Instagram that week.

Your WooCommerce orders already know who these people are. The cake message says "Happy 30th Anna" on 14 March. The same customer ordered on 12 March last year. That's a birthday you can plan for.

Questions

Does Loyalbase read the message written on the cake?

Only on your own website. The plugin turns the wording into a label such as birthday, anniversary, wedding or new baby, and only the label is sent. The wording itself never leaves your store.

We have walk-in customers at our branches. Are they included?

Not yet. Loyalbase reads online orders from WooCommerce today. Branch and POS sales are on the roadmap. Online orders picked up at a branch are included, and show the branch as the area.

Can I see which branch people pick up from?

Yes. When the shipping method says pickup or takeaway with a branch name, Loyalbase shows it as Pickup and the branch name, so you can ask for sales or customers by branch.

Many of our orders are gifts. Who gets the reminder?

The person who placed the order. Loyalbase marks an order as a gift when it has a gift tick, a card message, or a different name or address for delivery, and the reminder goes to the buyer.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
