For gift, flower and hamper shops

Remind gift buyers before the date, not after

Gift buyers order for someone else, on a date that comes round every year. Loyalbase spots the date, the gift and the buyer, and tells you who to remind first.

Updated 7 November 20264 min readBy the Loyalbase team
Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for gift, flower and hamper shops on WooCommerce (Shopify in early access). It recognises gift orders, learns the dates each customer buys for, groups company buyers together, and lists who to remind before Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, birthdays and hamper season, with a WhatsApp message for each. It also shows which of last year's peak-day buyers never came back.

Why gift customers drift away

A gift shop's best customer might order four times a year: a partner's birthday, an anniversary, Mother's Day and Valentine's Day. Each order is an emergency. If you're the first shop they think of, you get it. If not, it goes to whoever has the fastest delivery that day.

Peak days make this worse. Valentine's Day can bring hundreds of first-time buyers, and most never order again. That's not because they didn't like you. Nobody asked them back.

Questions

How does Loyalbase know an order was a gift?

An order counts as a gift when it has a gift tick or card message, or when the delivery name or address is different from the buyer's. That check runs on your website.

Does the person who received the gift get a message?

No. Loyalbase only works with your customer, the person who paid. Recipients' details aren't used.

Can it tell which occasion each order was for?

Yes, when your checkout has a card or gift message. The plugin turns the wording into a label such as birthday, anniversary, get well or thank you, and only the label is sent.

We sell hampers to companies. Can I see those customers separately?

Yes. Customers who order with a company name, or from a work email address, are marked as business customers, and people from the same company are counted together. Personal addresses like Gmail, Yahoo or Outlook aren't counted, and the email and domain never leave your store.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
