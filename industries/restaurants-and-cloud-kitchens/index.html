Keep your regulars ordering direct, not through the delivery apps

Every order on your own website saves you the delivery app's cut. Loyalbase shows who your direct regulars are, when they usually order, and who has gone quiet, so you can keep them.

Updated 7 November 20263 min readBy the Loyalbase team
Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for restaurants and cloud kitchens that take orders on their own WooCommerce website. It lists regulars who have gone quiet and first-time customers who didn't come back, shows the busiest days and hours, delivery against pickup and sales by area or branch, and writes a WhatsApp message for each customer. Orders placed through delivery apps aren't included.

Why direct regulars matter

Delivery apps bring new customers, then take a slice of every order those customers place. When the same person orders from your own website, you keep the margin and you know who they are.

Direct regulars are worth protecting. They usually order on the same days, at the same times, for the same dishes. When one goes quiet, you should hear about it in weeks, not months.

Questions

Does Loyalbase include orders from delivery apps?

No. It reads orders placed on your own WooCommerce website. Orders placed through delivery apps stay in those apps.

We take dine-in and walk-in orders on a POS. Are those included?

Not yet. Branch and POS sales are on our roadmap. Online orders for pickup at a branch are included and show the branch name.

Can I see busy times by day and hour?

Yes. Ask which day of the week sells most, or what time of day people order, for any period.

How do I message a regular?

Tap the customer and WhatsApp opens with the message written. You can change every word before it goes. Lists can also be downloaded as a WhatsApp campaign file, with only people who said yes.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
