Parents reorder nappies and wipes on a rhythm, then move to the next size, the next toy and the next birthday. Loyalbase shows who is due, and what parents usually buy next.

Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for baby and kids stores on WooCommerce (Shopify in early access). It learns each parent's reorder rhythm, lists who is due or has gone quiet, shows what new customers buy first and who comes back, spots gift orders like new baby gifts, and writes a WhatsApp message for each customer.

How parents buy

A baby store has a customer for a few years, and the needs change every few months. Newborn nappies become size 3. Bottles become cups. Then it's birthdays, school bags and Children's Day.

That means "stopped ordering" doesn't always mean lost. Sometimes the child simply grew out of what you sell them. The fix is to show them the next stage at the right time, before they find it somewhere else.

Questions

A customer stopped buying nappies. Have we lost them?

Not always. Often the child moved to a new size or stage. Loyalbase shows what they used to buy, so your message can offer the next stage instead of the same product.

Can it spot new baby gifts?

Yes, when the order has a gift tick, a card message or a different delivery address. The plugin turns card wording into a label such as new baby or birthday, and the wording never leaves your store.

Do you store information about children?

No. Loyalbase works with orders and the occasion label only. Customer names, phones, emails, addresses and card wording stay on your store.

Can I message only parents who said yes?

Yes. Bulk downloads and email sends only include customers who said yes to messages. Tapping to WhatsApp one person is your call.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
