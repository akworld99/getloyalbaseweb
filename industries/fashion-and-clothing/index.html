Bring last season's shoppers back for the next one

Fashion customers buy in seasons: Eid, Avurudu, Deepavali, Christmas, a big sale day. Loyalbase shows who bought last season and hasn't come back, so your launch goes to people who already like you.

Updated 7 November 20264 min readBy the Loyalbase team
Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for fashion and clothing stores on WooCommerce (Shopify in early access). It groups customers by the festivals they shop for, finds last season's buyers who haven't returned, shows who only buys with a discount code, and compares this season with last year's, with a WhatsApp message ready for each customer.

How fashion customers buy

Clothing doesn't run out like coffee. People come back when there's a reason: a festival, a wedding, a new collection, a sale. Miss the reason and they buy their festival outfit somewhere else.

The good news is the reasons repeat. A customer who bought before Eid two years running is very likely to buy before this Eid. You just need to reach her before she starts browsing.

Questions

How does Loyalbase decide someone is an Eid customer?

Each festival has a short ordering window, for example the 10 days before Eid ul-Fitr. Orders in that window count for that festival, year by year. Days that fall in two festivals' windows count for neither, so an order is never put down to the wrong one.

Which festivals does it know?

Customers are grouped by Christmas, Avurudu, Eid ul-Fitr, Eid ul-Adha, Vesak, Deepavali and Thai Pongal. Peak dates also cover Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, 11.11, Black Friday, New Year's Eve and more, and you can pick your own dates in Ask.

Can I see if my sale only attracted discount hunters?

Yes. Ask for customers who used a discount code in a period, and look at how many of them came back for a second order.

Does it handle sizes and variants?

Loyalbase works with product names as they appear on the order. Best sellers, slow sellers and bought together use those names.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
