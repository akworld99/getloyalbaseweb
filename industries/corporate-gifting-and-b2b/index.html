For corporate gifting and B2B orders

See your company customers as companies, not strangers

Five people from one company order from you, each with their own email. WooCommerce sees five strangers. Loyalbase sees five buyers from one company, what they spend and when they order.

Updated 7 November 20264 min readBy the Loyalbase team

Short answer

Loyalbase finds business customers in your WooCommerce orders from the company name at checkout, or from a work email address when the company field is empty. Personal addresses like Gmail, Yahoo and Outlook are left out, and the email and domain never leave your store. People from the same company are counted together, and you get a list of every business customer sorted by spend, who ordered last festive season but not this one, and who to call before year-end.

Why company customers get missed

Corporate orders are often a store's biggest: year-end hampers, staff birthday cakes, client gifts, office supplies. But they arrive looking like ordinary orders, placed by different people from the same office, often without a company name.

So the account is invisible. Nobody notices when the company that ordered 40 hampers last December hasn't called this year, until December is over.

How Loyalbase spots a company

  1. Company name at checkout. If the customer typed one, that's the company.
  2. Work email address. If the company field is empty, an email at a company's own domain counts. People at the same company are counted as one company.
  3. Personal and shop addresses left out. Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, Outlook, iCloud and similar, plus local internet providers and your own shop's domain, never count as a company.
  4. Nothing readable leaves your store. The plugin sends only a scrambled code for the company. Loyalbase can tell two people share a company, but can't turn the code back into the email or domain.

Questions

How does Loyalbase know a customer is a business?

From the company name at checkout, or from a work email address when that's empty. Personal providers like Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, Outlook and iCloud don't count, and nor does your own shop's domain.

Does my customers' email domain leave my store?

No. The plugin sends a scrambled code made with a key that stays on your website. Loyalbase can tell when two customers share a company, but can't read the email or domain.

Do I need to update the plugin?

Yes, to group by work email you need plugin version 0.5.0 or newer. Settings in Loyalbase has a download for the latest version once your store is connected.

Is this a CRM?

No. Loyalbase shows who your company customers are, what they're worth and who to call, from your orders. If you use a CRM, keep it.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
