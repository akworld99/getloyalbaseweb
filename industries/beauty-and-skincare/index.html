Reach beauty customers when the bottle runs out

Skincare customers come back when they run out, if they remember you. Loyalbase learns each customer's refill rhythm and shows which first products turn into regulars.

Updated 7 November 20264 min readBy the Loyalbase team
Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for beauty and skincare brands on WooCommerce (Shopify in early access). It learns how often each customer reorders, lists who is overdue, shows which first product leads to a second order, what's bought together and whether discount codes bring people back, with a WhatsApp message ready for each customer.

How beauty customers come back

A cleanser lasts about as long as it lasts. When it runs out, your customer either reorders from you, picks something up at the pharmacy, or tries the brand an influencer posted that morning. The order goes to whoever is in front of them at the right moment.

Two things decide most of it: reaching people near their refill date, and knowing which first product makes someone stay. Loyalbase answers both from your own orders.

Questions

Can it tell me which product brings customers back?

Yes. Ask "What new customers buy first, and who comes back" to see each first product with how many of those customers ordered again.

Should I offer a discount to bring people back?

Check first. Ask for orders with and without a discount code, or customers who used a code. Loyalbase shows which codes bring people back for a second order and which only bring one-off buyers.

Does it connect to my Instagram and Facebook?

Yes. Connect Facebook to see Meta ad results and your Facebook and Instagram posts next to the sales that really came in. Ask can rank your posts by reach, likes or comments.

Can I send launch news to my best customers only?

Yes. Ask for your top customers by spend, then download the list as a WhatsApp campaign file or send it to Sendy. Only people who said yes are included.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
