Notice the missed weekly shop before it becomes a lost customer

Grocery customers don't cancel. They miss one week, then two, then they're shopping somewhere else. Loyalbase shows you who missed their usual shop while it's still easy to win them back.

Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for online grocery stores and supermarkets on WooCommerce (Shopify in early access). It learns how often each customer shops, lists who is due and who has gone quiet, shows sales by delivery area, day and time, and gives you a WhatsApp message for each person. For weekly shoppers, Ask can find anyone with a month since their last order.

How grocery customers slip away

Your best grocery customers shop every week or two. The basket is big and the margin is thin, so each lost regular costs you real money every month. They rarely complain. A late delivery, one missing item, a cheaper app, and they quietly move on.

The earlier you notice, the cheaper it is to win them back. A message in week three feels like service. A message in month three feels like spam.

The lists that matter most for a grocery store

Late regular

Regulars who missed their usual shop

Customers with two or more orders who have gone clearly longer than their own gap. For weekly shoppers, ask for anyone with 4+ orders and nothing in a month.

First time buyer

First order, no second

The second order is where grocery habits start. Bought once, 45 to 150 days ago, and not back.

Unpaid checkout

Baskets that stopped at payment

Full baskets that didn't get paid in the last two weeks. Often a card problem, easy to fix with a quick message.

Best customer

Big-basket households

Customers whose usual order is well above average. Worth a personal thank you and a priority delivery slot.

Questions

Our customers shop weekly. Will the late list flag them in time?

The late list waits at least 45 days, so it never nags. For weekly shoppers, use Ask: Customers who ordered 4+ times but haven't ordered in a month. Change the numbers to suit your store.

Can it show which delivery areas are slipping?

Yes. Sales by delivery area shows orders and sales for each area, and you can compare any two periods. Customers in an area can be listed and sorted by spend.

Does it work with my delivery slots plugin?

Loyalbase reads your orders after they're placed and doesn't change checkout, so it works alongside slot and delivery plugins.

Can I send one message to everyone on a list?

You can download any list as a WhatsApp campaign file for Meta's Messaging campaigns, a spreadsheet, or send it to your Sendy email list. Only people who said yes to messages are included.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
