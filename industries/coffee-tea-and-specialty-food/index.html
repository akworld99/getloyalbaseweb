Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for coffee, tea and specialty food brands on WooCommerce (Shopify in early access). It learns each customer's reorder rhythm, shows which first product leads to a second order, separates gift orders from everyday ones, groups office buyers by company, and gives you a WhatsApp message for each customer.

How coffee and tea customers come back

Your customers buy for two reasons. They drink it every day and reorder when the bag runs low. And they give it as a gift, to family at festivals or to clients at year-end. The everyday buyer is your steady income. The gift buyer brings you new everyday buyers.

Most brands treat them the same. Loyalbase splits them, so the reorder reminder goes to the daily drinker and the hamper reminder goes to the gifter.

A message that works for coffee and tea

First time buyer

From tasting set to full bag

When to send: about four weeks after a first order of a sample or tasting set.

Hi [Name], how did you find the tasting set? Most people end up with a favourite. Tell me which one and I'll send a full bag, roasted this week. [Shop name]

Why it works: It asks for their opinion first, and turns the answer into an easy order. "Roasted this week" gives a reason to buy direct.

Questions

We sell subscriptions. Is Loyalbase still useful?

Yes. It shows customers who buy regularly without a subscription, and those whose orders stopped. Those are the people most likely to sign up, or to need a nudge.

Can I find customers who buy for gifts?

Yes. Orders with a gift tick, card message or a different delivery name or address count as gifts. Ask for customers who mostly order gifts, or compare gift and other orders.

Can I see how one product is doing over time?

Yes. Ask how any product sold, month by month, and which products are rising or falling.

Does it work outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. Loyalbase works in any country and currency, and phone numbers are matched in full international form.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
