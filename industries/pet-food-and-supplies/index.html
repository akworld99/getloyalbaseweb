Remind pet owners before the food bag runs out

Pet food is the most predictable reorder in ecommerce. The same food, the same bag size, every few weeks. Loyalbase learns each customer's timing and shows who is due before they run out.

Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for pet food and pet supply stores on WooCommerce (Shopify in early access). It learns how often each customer reorders, lists who is due in the next few days and who has gone past their usual gap, and shows which products are bought together, with a WhatsApp reminder ready for each customer.

How pet owners reorder

A dog doesn't skip dinner. When the bag runs low, the owner buys more, from you or from the supermarket on the way home. Once they switch, they rarely switch back, because pets get used to a food and owners stop shopping around.

That makes timing everything. A reminder two days before the bag runs out wins the order. A reminder two weeks after means they already bought it elsewhere.

A message that works for pet food

Late regular

The bag is about to run out

When to send: a few days before a regular is due, or as soon as they go past their usual gap.

Hi [Name], it's about time for [pet's food] again. Want us to send the usual [bag size] on [day]? Reply YES and we'll sort it. [Shop name]

Why it works: One word to reply, and the decision is already made for them. Reminders that save a trip work better than discounts.

Questions

Can Loyalbase send reminders automatically?

Not yet. Today it lists who is due and writes the message, and you send it with a tap on WhatsApp. Automatic reminders that you approve first are next on our roadmap.

How does it know when a customer is due?

It looks at the gaps between each customer's own past orders. Someone who orders every 30 days is due around day 30. Ask can list everyone due in the next few days.

Can I see customers by the product they buy?

Yes. Ask for customers who bought a product in any period, or who bought it but haven't ordered since a set number of days.

We also sell in a physical shop. Are those sales included?

Not yet. Online orders from WooCommerce are included today. POS and branch sales are on the roadmap.

Does Loyalbase see my customers' names and phone numbers?

No. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your store. Loyalbase holds order facts and scrambled codes it can't turn back into contact details. When you open a customer, your browser loads their name straight from your store.
