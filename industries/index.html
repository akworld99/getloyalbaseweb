Don't see your industry?

If your customers buy more than once, Loyalbase will find the ones drifting away. It doesn't need to know your industry, because it judges each customer against their own past orders. A customer who buys every three weeks is late after about six. One who buys twice a year isn't late for about nine months.

Some features shine for certain stores. Occasions come from gift card and cake wording. Festival groups suit stores with seasonal peaks. Company accounts suit anyone who sells to offices. Ask works for everyone.

Questions

Which stores is Loyalbase best for?

Stores where customers buy more than once: food, gifts, beauty, fashion, pet, coffee, baby and kids, and anyone selling to offices. One-off purchases like furniture get less from it.

Does it work outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. Loyalbase works in any country and currency. Phone numbers are matched in full international form.

Which platforms does it work with?

WooCommerce today. The Shopify app is in early access. POS and branch sales are on the roadmap.
