About Loyalbase

We help store owners get customers to order again, without a loyalty program, and prove what came back.

Updated 7 November 2026
Short answer

Loyalbase is a customer retention app for WooCommerce and Shopify stores, built in Colombo, Sri Lanka, by Digisavvy, a performance marketing team. It reads a store's orders, tells the owner who to message each day and why, and shows the sales that came back.

How it started

Loyalbase began as a customer dashboard we built for a cake and gifting store in Sri Lanka. The owner wanted to know which regulars had gone quiet and who to remind before birthdays and anniversaries. We turned it into a product any WooCommerce or Shopify store can use.

What we believe

  • Customer data belongs to the store. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses stay on your website. Loyalbase works from order facts and scrambled IDs.
  • Plain words beat dashboards. "Late for their next order" instead of "at risk segment". One clear thing to do on every screen.
  • Proof beats vanity numbers. We count the orders your messages added, against a held back group, not messages sent or opened.
  • WhatsApp first. In many markets, customers answer WhatsApp long before they open an email.

Who we are

Loyalbase is built by Digisavvy, a performance marketing team in Colombo, and founded by Akmal Afzal.

Where we're going

Next up: the Shopify app, automatic WhatsApp reminders you approve before they send, and walk in sales from POS systems at your outlets. Our first markets are Sri Lanka, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, the UAE and the Gulf, and stores anywhere can join.
