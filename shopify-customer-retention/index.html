Short answer

Loyalbase for Shopify is in early access. It reads your Shopify orders, finds customers who are late for their next order, have a yearly occasion coming up or bought once and never came back, and gives you a WhatsApp message for each. Join early access and we'll set your store up as soon as it can connect.

What Shopify stores get

ListWho is on it
Late regularsCustomers with two or more orders who have gone quiet for longer than their usual gap.
Yearly occasionsCustomers who order around the same date every year, due in the next two weeks.
Unpaid checkoutsPeople who picked what they wanted in the last two weeks and didn't finish paying.
First time buyersBought once, 45 to 150 days ago. The window when a second order is most likely.
Stopped orderingTwo or more orders in the past, nothing in over a year.

Plus the rest of Loyalbase: sales by returning and new customers, dates to plan for with last year's numbers, Meta ads and posts next to real sales, and Results with a held back group so you know what your messages added.

How it's different from a Shopify loyalty app

Points apps such as Smile.io, LoyaltyLion and Yotpo reward customers who keep coming back. That's useful, but a customer who has stopped buying doesn't visit your store, so they never see their points.

Loyalbase goes after the ones who stopped. There's no widget to install, no rewards to design and no discounts to fund. You get a short list each morning and a message to send. Many stores run both. Read loyalty program or win back messages for the full comparison.

Privacy on Shopify

Loyalbase works from order facts and scrambled IDs, not contact details. On Shopify, customer names load inside your Shopify admin, and are never stored by Loyalbase.
